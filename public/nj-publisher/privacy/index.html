NJ Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

NJ Publisher is a private tool built and used only by Niraj V (NJ) to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channels (Unboxing with NJ, NJ Stories) and Instagram accounts (@heyitsnjtech, @heyitsnjstories). It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): channel name and ID, and the ability to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, schedules and privacy settings, and delete videos on the connected channel.
  • Instagram / Facebook (Meta Graph API): the linked Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts, and the ability to publish reels and posts to them.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the macOS Keychain. Videos and their details are read from the owner's computer and sent only to YouTube or Instagram when publishing. No data is collected from other people, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

NJ Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Meta

Access to Instagram and Facebook can be removed at any time from Facebook Settings → Business integrations.

Deleting data

Revoking access as above stops all use. The owner can also delete the stored tokens from the Keychain at any time.
